- After restart, broker health returned `{"ok":true,"service":"cos-control-broker","version":"0.3.0"}`.

- Security/unit suite: 23/23 passed in the broker image with network disabled.
- Host `py_compile`, `git diff --check`, and `docker compose config -q`: PASS.

## Scope and limitations

- Live cross-identity round-trip is proven for WS1 and WS3 only. WS2 and WS4-WS8 are not yet enrolled.
- Mailbox delivery is pull-based. It does not auto-wake a ChatGPT tab, inject messages into another conversation, or execute a recipient agent automatically.
- Existing owner-scoped `agent_*` permissions were not relaxed. A2A does not address or inspect another owner's COS sessions.
